Data Dictionary & Metric Logic
Portfolio Contracted ARR
The point-in-time active baseline of booked revenue. The pipeline automatically deduplicates overlapping or superseded mid-year expansions to prevent artificial inflation of the baseline.
Activated ARR (aARR)
Contracted revenue associated with accounts that have consumed 50% or more of their prorated 90-day compute credits, or are currently in the 45-day onboarding grace period.
Unactivated Risk ARR
Revenue associated with accounts falling below the 50% activation threshold. These accounts are mathematically modeled as high churn risks.
CSM Pulse vs System Status
CSM Pulse represents the qualitative legacy health score input by a rep. System Status represents the quantitative mathematical reality of credit consumption. The Agent searches for contradictions between the two.
Technical Health (Derived)
Technical health (support tickets) is mathematically derived from the CSM Pulse rating. Red pulse indicates active Sev-1 blocker tickets; Green pulse indicates zero active critical tickets.
